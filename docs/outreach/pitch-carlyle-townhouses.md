**Subject:** 42 small Brooklyn buildings, ten LLCs, one Washington address on the deeds

Hi [name],

Since April 2025, ten limited liability companies have taken title to 42 small
apartment buildings, almost all in Brooklyn. Thirty-eight of them were bought
from outside sellers for about $197 million. They are three-family walk-ups and
six-unit pre-war buildings in Park Slope, Boerum Hill, Bushwick, Greenpoint,
Red Hook, Bed-Stuy, Williamsburg and Prospect Heights: 202 apartments in total,
35 of the 42 built before 1974.

Seven of those companies are called TOWNHOUSE RENTAL, with a roman numeral
after it. Most of their deeds give a filing address at 93 4th Avenue in
Manhattan. Five party rows do not. They give **THE CARLYLE GROUP, Washington DC
20004**, which is the ZIP of Carlyle's headquarters. Two more buyers in the same
set, BROOKLYN TOWNHOUSE PROPERTY OWNER III and IV LLC, file from that same
Washington address, and a third, 202 8TH STREET OWNER LLC, filed care of The
Carlyle Group in Manhattan when it paid $32.1 million for 438 4th Avenue in
Park Slope.

The interesting part is not that a fund bought a building. It is what kind of
building. Institutional money in New York usually shows up in towers. This is
the small-building stock, the three and six unit walk-ups that hold the
unregulated end of the rental market, being assembled one LLC at a time.

I run PulseCities, which reassembles NYC deed records into the operations
behind them. The eight TOWNHOUSE RENTAL companies and 38 of the buildings are
at https://pulsecities.com/network/townhouse-rental. The other four (438 4th
Avenue, 333 Warren Street, 238 Hall Street and 557 17th Street) were bought by
the two adjacent names and are in the table below.

Three things I want to be straight about, because they shape the story:

1. **The deeds do not say Carlyle owns these.** An ACRIS party address is what
   the filer typed. It names The Carlyle Group as a care-of address on those
   rows and nothing more. The second source is a New York Department of State
   entity search, or asking Carlyle.
2. **Nothing bad has happened in these buildings on the record.** Zero executed
   marshal evictions across all 42, and 47 open HPD or DOB violations between
   them, which is low for 42 pre-war buildings. This is a story about who is
   buying, not about harm.
3. **Nine of the deeds move a building from one of these LLCs to another.**
   Those are restructurings, not purchases, and they are not in the $197M.

Happy to send the full list with BBLs, prices and deed IDs so you can pull each
one from ACRIS yourself.

## Before you send

1. **Check whether Carlyle has said anything publicly.** A fund vehicle for
   small-multifamily rentals may well have been announced. If it has, the story
   is what it bought and where, not that it exists.
2. **Confirm the entities.** A NY Department of State entity search on
   TOWNHOUSE RENTAL II LLC and BROOKLYN TOWNHOUSE PROPERTY OWNER IV LLC gives
   the registered agent and service address, which is the second source.
3. **Do not say Carlyle evicted anyone or neglected anything.** The record shows
   no evictions and few violations. Getting that wrong once costs the
   relationship permanently.
4. The follow-up a reporter will ask for is the full list with BBLs. Export it
   from /network/townhouse-rental or ask for a CSV.
