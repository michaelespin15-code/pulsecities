## Before you send

1. **The deeds transfer real estate, not operators.** A nursing home's DOH
   operating certificate moves through a separate approval, and the landlord
   and the operator are often different companies on purpose. Do not write that
   an operator changed hands. Ask DOH.
2. **The deeds do not name a person.** Nine LLCs filing from one Lakewood
   address is a fact about filings. Who controls them is a New Jersey and New
   York entity search, plus CMS ownership disclosures.
3. **Get the facility names from DOH, not from me.** The deed record carries
   addresses and BBLs and no facility names, so I have not asserted any. CMS
   Care Compare and the DOH nursing home profiles are keyed on address, which
   is how to match them.
4. **Check for prior coverage.** A half-billion-dollar portfolio may already
   have been covered when it closed in March.
