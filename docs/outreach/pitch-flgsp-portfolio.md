## Before you send

1. **Check whether this is already reported.** A $451M portfolio trade may well
   have been covered by The Real Deal or Crain's when it closed in spring. If it
   was, the pitch changes from "here is a deal" to "here is what the deal was
   carrying, building by building", which is still a story nobody has written
   but is a different email.
2. **Do not identify who is behind FLGSP.** The deeds give the name and the
   filing addresses and nothing more. Say what the record says.
3. **Do not imply the buyer evicted anyone.** 98 of the 99 evictions predate the
   sale. Getting this wrong once costs the relationship permanently.
4. The follow-up a reporter will ask for is the full 82-row list. Export it from
   /network/flgsp or ask for a CSV.
