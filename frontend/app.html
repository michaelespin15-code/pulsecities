How scores work

PulseCities scores each NYC ZIP using six public-record signals, normalized by residential units and refreshed nightly.

  • LLC acquisitions
  • Permit filings
  • 311 complaints
  • Evictions
  • HPD violations
  • Rent-stabilized unit loss

Higher scores mean more displacement-pressure signals. They are not predictions about any individual tenant or building.

Sources include NYC DOB, HPD, ACRIS, 311, eviction records, DHCR, and MapPLUTO.
